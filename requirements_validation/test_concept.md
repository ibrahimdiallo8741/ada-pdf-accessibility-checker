Representative test data will come from a shared set of webpages and PDFs created or selected by the pod to include valid cases, known accessibility violations, invalid inputs, and boundary cases such as a 25 MB PDF or unreachable URL. Expected outputs will be established by manually reviewing the fixtures against the supported WCAG 2.1 criteria and documenting which findings should be Pass, Issue, Needs Human Review, or Could Not Evaluate. To keep evaluation repeatable, the pod will use fixed test files and unchanged webpage snapshots where possible, and external services will be mocked or tested under controlled conditions. Because AI results may vary, deterministic findings will be compared exactly, while AI-assisted results will be evaluated using predefined expectations and will remain clearly labeled as AI-generated.
